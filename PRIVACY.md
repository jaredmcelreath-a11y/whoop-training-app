# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

This is a personal training app that connects to WHOOP to help plan training for a half Ironman.

## What data the app uses
With your permission, the app reads the following WHOOP data: your profile, body measurements, workouts, sleep, recovery and daily cycles (strain).

## How the data is used
The data is used only to plan and adjust training inside this app.

## Storage and sharing
The data is stored privately. It is never sold and never shared with third parties.

## Revoking access
You can revoke the app's access at any time from the WHOOP app or by disconnecting inside this app. When access is revoked, the app stops receiving new data.
